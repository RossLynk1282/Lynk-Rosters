How to read this roster ShowHide

The grid. Each column is a day, split into sections — Dispatch, Customer Care, Supervisor, Operators, and Swift Limerick on Sundays. Colours match the legend above the grid (Dispatch, Customer Care, Supervisor, Operators, Swift Limerick). A small "X gigs" badge on a day means a big concert is on nearby that night, so expect busier traffic.

Find your own shifts. Tap or click your name — on any shift card, or in the "Full Team" list. All of your shifts for the week get a gold outline, and your full weekly schedule (shift count, days on, times) appears in the panel. Tap your name again to clear it.

Filter by role. The "Show" buttons (All Roles / Dispatch / Customer Care / Supervisor / Operators) hide everything except that department — handy for checking who's covering a role on a given day.

Festival Watch. Lists each night's gigs and venues so everyone knows when to expect a surge in demand.
